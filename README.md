 Customer Marketing Analysis Dashboard

 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer marketing data to identify customer behavior, campaign performance, spending patterns, and business opportunities. The dashboard helps stakeholders make data-driven marketing decisions by visualizing key performance indicators (KPIs) and customer insights.

---

 Objectives

* Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
* Measure marketing campaign effectiveness.
* Identify high-value customer segments.
* Track revenue and customer spending trends.
* Discover factors influencing customer purchases.

---

 Tools Used
python google collab 
---
 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

 Total Customers
Average Customer Income
Average Spending
Average Customer Age
Total Purchases
Campaign Acceptance Rate

---

 Dashboard Features

* Customer Demographics Analysis
* Income vs Spending Analysis
* Purchase Distribution by Product Category
* Campaign Performance Analysis
* Customer Segmentation
* Marital Status & Education Insights
* Country/Region Analysis (if available)
* Interactive Filters & Dynamic KPIs

---

 Business Insights

* Identify the most valuable customer segments.
* Understand which campaigns drive the highest conversions.
* Compare spending across customer groups.
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
* Support data-driven marketing strategies.
